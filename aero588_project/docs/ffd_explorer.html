What a control point does

Every surface point is tied to fixed coordinates (u, v) inside the box. When a control point moves by Δ, a surface point moves by Ni(u) · Nj(v) · Δ: the chordwise basis value times the share of the lower or upper row. The motion is linear in Δ, which is why pyGeo's geometric constraints are linear in the shape variables.

Why the move is local

A B-spline of order k makes each basis function non-zero over at most k knot spans. With cubic splines (order 4) and 10 stations, one control point reshapes about 40 % of the chord and leaves the rest exactly unchanged. Order 2 gives hat functions with sharp kinks; fewer stations give broad, smooth changes.

Global versus local

Incidence rotates the whole box about c/4 and ride height lifts it. pyGeo applies these first, then adds the local Δy along global y. The LE/TE constraint keeps the end pairs moving equal and opposite, so local variables cannot sneak in a rotation or a lift that duplicates the global ones.

The regulation box is a constraint

The section starts with its lowest point exactly on the 0.20 m floor, so the floor is active from the first move: the lower surface cannot go down until the section is lifted (ride height, or "Seat on the floor"). Each surface coordinate is linear in a control-point move, so a drag stops exactly at the first contact. In the optimization this becomes a set of linear inequality constraints on the lower- and upper-surface points.

Why the section cannot turn inside out

At a given x the upper and lower surface points share the same u, so the local thickness is (vupper − vlower) · Σ Ni(u) gi, where gi is the vertical gap between station i's two control points. The first factor is fixed and positive and the Ni are never negative. So if every gi stays above gmin, the thickness is positive everywhere, not only at the sampled stations. Turn both constraints off and drag a pair together near the trailing edge to see the loop cross itself and the signed area drop.

The FFD box has to contain the section

The box edge is the B-spline curve, not the control polygon, and it cuts inside the polygon's corners. With few stations a 2 % c margin is not enough and the section would poke out, which pyGeo cannot embed. The explorer then widens the margin and reports the value it used.

Global variables

Regulation box

The section must stay inside this box. Drags, sliders and presets stop where the surface touches an edge. The floor doubles as the clearance limit.

Shape constraints

Both are linear in the control-point moves, so drags stop exactly at the limit. Horizontal moves only check the regulation box.
