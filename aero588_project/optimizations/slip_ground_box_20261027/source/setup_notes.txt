SETUP CHECKS AND NUMERICAL CHOICE

1. The original slip-ground mesh contains 88,548 cells before overset blanking.
2. A preliminary full-overset attempt emitted ADflow's warning that full-update mesh derivatives are not implemented. It was stopped before accepting optimization results.
3. A fast-update attempt passed a small incidence derivative screen but later stopped when a trial produced invalid interpolation weights for its fixed donors.
4. Full-primal/fast-local-adjoint trials could generate orphan cells after small geometry changes.
5. Extending the near-body grid from 81 to 101 normal nodes and from 0.025 to 0.04 m did not eliminate full-update failures. Near-wall protection and donor-priority tests did not cure them. This test mesh is not used for the reported optimization.
6. Diagnostics located orphan cells at the outer edge of the near-body grid. Failed setup trials are not optimized results.
7. The installed ADflow documentation, doc/options.yaml under oversetUpdateMode, describes frozen connectivity as an approach for the entire overset mesh warped together with IDWarp USMesh. This is the chosen production setup on the original mesh.
8. Both primal and adjoint computations use frozen connectivity/weights, so their discrete models match. This introduces an interpolation approximation under deformation, to be considered alongside ordinary discretization error.
9. The production archive's source/aerodynamic_derivative_check.json and source/geometry_derivative_check.json record checks for the chosen setup. The production run.log contains its complete solver output.
10. No grid-independence study has been completed. Solver convergence and optimizer termination must be interpreted separately.
